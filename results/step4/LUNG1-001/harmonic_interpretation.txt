FOURIER HARMONIC INTERPRETATION
========================================

1 harmonic:
Very coarse representation of the overall shape.

2-4 harmonics:
Capture increasingly important global shape characteristics.

8-16 harmonics:
Represent major shape variations and moderate boundary irregularities.

32 harmonics:
Retain substantially more local boundary detail.

Full Fourier representation:
Reconstructs the sampled boundary with minimal truncation loss.
