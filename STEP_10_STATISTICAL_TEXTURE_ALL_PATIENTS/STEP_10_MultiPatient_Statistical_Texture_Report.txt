FORMULAS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mean:
m = sum[z_i * p(z_i)]

Variance:
sigma^2 = sum[(z_i - m)^2 * p(z_i)]

Normalized variance:
sigma_norm^2 = sigma^2 / (intensity_range)^2

Smoothness:
R = 1 - 1/(1 + sigma_norm^2)

Third moment:
mu_3 = sum[(z_i - m)^3 * p(z_i)]

Uniformity:
U = sum[p(z_i)^2]

Entropy:
e = -sum[p(z_i) * log2(p(z_i))]
